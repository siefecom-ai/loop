What it does

Every deck starts from one sentence. Loop handles the rest.

AI generates slides

Type "the causes of World War I" and Loop writes a 10-slide deck — title slide, sections, content, and a conclusion.

Themes that look designed

Modern, Executive, Editorial, Minimal. Each theme picks its own colors and fonts, so every deck looks intentional.

Templates for anything

Business pitch, school project, book report, science study. Start from a proven structure and let AI fill it in.

Edit any slide

Tap edit and change the title or any bullet. Loop re-renders the slide instantly.

Everything on your phone

Your projects stay on your device. Loop only receives the topic you type, to write the slides.

Fast, quiet, minimal

No splash screens, no ads, no clutter. Just the tool you need to get a presentation done.
